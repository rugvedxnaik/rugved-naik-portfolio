BAJA SAE India 2023 / Team leadership / Vehicle delivery

Building the Team Before Building the Vehicle

How I grew a five-person engineering group into a 25-person agile vehicle organization and led it like a startup.

Real project. Captain and Head of Engineering Design, Team Abhedya, BAJA SAE India 2023.

Role

Captain and Head of Engineering Design, responsible for overall vehicle design and ATV vehicle dynamics.

Team

Built the group from 5 people to 24 members plus me, organized across five vehicle subsystems and business functions.

Constraint

Four months to deliver work normally planned across nine, after COVID and competition-date changes.

Resources

INR 1.75M budget, approximately EUR 19K at 2023 rates, and INR 0.8M secured in sponsorships.

Decision

Moved to a new chain-driven 4WD assembly when resource constraints made the six-month vehicle-dynamics design impractical.

Result

Team Abhedya ranked 15th overall at BAJA SAEINDIA 2023 and 4th overall at ATVC 2023.

The challenge

This was closer to building a startup than completing a college project. The product was an all-terrain vehicle, but delivery depended on recruiting a team, defining ownership, funding the work, resolving technical dependencies and keeping every subsystem moving against a compressed deadline.

How the organization worked

I expanded the original five-person group into a 25-person agile organization. Five departments owned the vehicle subsystems, supported by marketing, cost and finance, sponsorships and other delivery functions. As captain, I created the operating rhythm and kept technical and commercial work connected. As Head of Engineering Design, I remained accountable for overall design coherence and vehicle dynamics.

The technical trade-off

A six-month vehicle-dynamics subsystem had already absorbed significant design effort. Resource constraints made that route too risky for the revised deadline. We chose a completely new chain-driven assembly for 4WD instead. It was not the comfortable decision. It was the decision that gave the whole vehicle the best chance of being delivered.

The setback

COVID disruption and changing competition dates compressed a nine-month delivery cycle into four months. The response was not simply to work faster. We had to reduce coordination friction, clarify interfaces, make trade-offs earlier and treat sponsorship, cost and manufacturing as part of the product system.

Result and learning

Result: Team Abhedya ranked 15th overall at BAJA SAEINDIA 2023 and 4th overall at ATVC 2023.

Learning: product leadership is often the work of building the team, decision system and constraints around the product before the product itself can succeed.
