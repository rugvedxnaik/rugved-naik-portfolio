Product leadership / Operations / Competition proof

The machine had to be engineered. The belief around it had to be built.

BAJA SAE India was a technical vehicle project, but the real operating challenge was wider: align a 25-member team, secure resources, make proof visible, and carry the vehicle through national competition pressure.

Evidence base

Team captain and head of design across research and development, vehicle dynamics, production, simulation, sponsorships, and operations.

Retainable line

Technical work travels further when people can read the proof.

Team

25-member multidisciplinary build

Proof

Top national finishes and BAJA Oregon selection

System

Design, funding, logistics, and team narrative

Signal

I believe the machine when the team makes the proof visible.

Selected as one of three Indian teams to represent India at BAJA Oregon 2023.
01

Observation

A technical product is also a trust object.

The ATV had to perform in harsh terrain, but it also had to convince judges, sponsors, teammates, and institutional stakeholders that the work was serious. Vehicle design, simulation, production, funding, and competition logistics became one connected system.

Why it mattered

Performance was judged on the track, but belief was built before the vehicle moved.

Technical proof

Vehicle dynamics, manufacturing, simulation, testing, and harsh-terrain performance.

Stakeholder proof

Fundraising, team alignment, outreach, visibility, and competition readiness.

02

Tension

Engineering excellence does not explain itself.

A strong build can still be under-read if the proof is locked inside the workshop. The leadership task was to make the team's capability visible enough to win sponsorship, motivate execution, and stand up in evaluation.

Evidence

Secured INR 600,000 in sponsorships and coordinated competition logistics across a national field of 100+ engineering institutions.

“The work was not only to build the vehicle. It was to make the system around the vehicle hold.”

03

System

The output was a cross-functional operating system.

The work connected design decisions with proof moments: how the vehicle was built, how the team was funded, how priorities were managed, and how the story of the build could be understood outside the engineering team.

Product

Design, prototype, test, and optimize the ATV for performance conditions.

Operations

Coordinate funding, logistics, timelines, production, and evaluation readiness.

Narrative

Make the team's proof legible to sponsors, judges, and the wider campus community.

Result

AIR 6 in BAJA SAE India Phase 1 and Design Evaluation, AIR 4 at the Aravalli Terrain Vehicle Championship, and the Suspension and Traction Award.

04

What it proves

Consumer centric thinking also applies to technical products.

The question is still human: who needs to believe, what proof do they need, and where does the product have to become understandable before it becomes persuasive?
