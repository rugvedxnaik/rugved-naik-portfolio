Luxury beauty strategy Givenchy / 2026

Face Architecture

A beauty territory built by translating couture construction into a scalable system of product, ritual, and expression.

Opened from signal 028

Givenchy owned couture. It did not yet own the face.

Observation

Givenchy owned couture, but not an equally ownable beauty idea.

Tension

Heritage alone creates prestige. It does not automatically create a territory consumers can recognize and return to.

System

Face Architecture reframed beauty as something constructed with structure, personalization, and seasonal evolution.

World

The system moved across palette design, consultation, digital experience, and runway-linked refreshes.

Outcome

Shows territory building, luxury semiotics, and how strategy becomes a world instead of a slogan.

What the category missed

Heritage was being mistaken for territory.

What changed

Couture stopped behaving like reference and started behaving like system.

Brand creation / ritual design 19h03 / 2026

19h03

An evening ritual product built from the observation that the first drink is really about transition, permission, and presence.

Opened from signal 029

The first drink signals arrival before it signals flavor.

Observation

The first drink of the evening is rarely about the liquid alone. It marks a shift in tempo and identity.

Tension

Functional beverages speak wellness. Aperitifs speak indulgence. The transition ritual between them is strategically under-owned.

System

19h03 became a Paris-born functional sparkling aperitif organized around composed sociability.

World

Dusk palette, brass cues, terrace ritual, and a product language built for urban evenings rather than generic wellness.

Outcome

Shows occasion-led positioning, behavioral insight, and how a single observation can become a complete brand world.

What the category missed

Evening ritual was framed as liquid choice instead of state change.

What changed

The product owned a moment rather than a flavor profile.

Fragrance strategy / PMM L'Oréal Brandstorm / 2026

The Permanence Paradox

When dupes flatten material difference, luxury needs a moat rooted in memory, imprint, and emotional persistence.

Opened from signal 021

The next luxury moat is not the bottle. It is the memory the bottle creates.

Observation

A dupe can copy scent notes, packaging, and visual codes.

Tension

If imitation closes the gap at the level of product cues, luxury needs to compete on a deeper layer of meaning.

System

The answer was a fragrance memory architecture: a system built around imprint formation, archive logic, and emotional persistence.

World

A concept deck imagining perfume not as a bottle to compare, but as a memory system a dupe can never truly enter.

Outcome

Shows narrative differentiation, strategic reframing, and a PMM-like ability to define a moat instead of a message.

Thesis

Memory cannot be duped.

The next luxury battleground is not the bottle. It is the memory the bottle creates.
What the category missed

Dupes were being treated as parity at product level, not vulnerability at meaning level.

What changed

The competitive frame moved from product comparison to imprint architecture.

Editorial analysis Search Style Report / 2026

Search Style Report

An editorial object that made search behavior feel readable, current, and visually desirable rather than trapped inside a spreadsheet.

Opened from signal 017

Search is no longer neutral plumbing. It edits what people find legible.

Observation

Search results shape taste and discovery. They no longer behave like neutral utility alone.

Tension

The work of SEO is strategic, but most of its outputs feel visually dead and culturally distant.

System

The report translated SEO patterns into an editorial magazine with hierarchy, sequencing, and point of view.

World

Search data became cover lines, spreads, and designed reading rather than dashboard furniture.

Outcome

Shows information design, editorial framing, and the ability to make analysis itself feel like a product.

What the category missed

SEO outputs were being treated as operational, not editorial.

What changed

Analysis became something you could read, circulate, and remember.

Method

How the system forms.

The work is not linear in practice, but the logic is consistent. Observation creates the entry point. System design makes the insight usable.

01

Observation

I look for what the brand, product, or behavior is signaling unintentionally. Usually the useful thing is sitting just beneath the official brief.

02

Tension

An observation matters only when it exposes a contradiction: what people want but cannot articulate, or what the category assumes but no longer earns.

03

System

I turn that tension into a structure someone can actually own. A territory. A ritual. A message architecture. A product logic. A designed answer.

04

World

The system becomes real through language, image, interface, experience, or editorial form. If people cannot feel it, the strategy is unfinished.
