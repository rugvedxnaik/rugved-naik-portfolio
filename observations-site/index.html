Point of view

Consumer centric is not soft. It is operational.

A product decision asks what someone is trying to get done, signal, avoid, or become. A marketing decision asks what they should remember after the touchpoint disappears.

The case matters, but the consumer signal comes first. The company appears only after the behavior has been read and routed into a usable system.

What I read

A lens before the work.

01 / collection

Some people do not stay loyal to a fragrance. They build a collection that says who they are becoming.

02 / identity

Beauty is not only applied. It is assembled through planes, edges, colors, and permission.

03 / category noise

When every brand says protein, gut health, and better-for-you, stated preference becomes crowded evidence.

04 / memory

AI can personalize the journey, but brand meaning has to stay stable enough to be remembered.

05 / infrastructure

Shared data can improve service, but the front-of-house behavior still has to feel specific to the house.

06 / ritual

People often choose the object that helps them cross into the next version of the day.

Method

The method keeps the work readable.

  1. 01Observe

    Find the behavior, ritual, review pattern, search language, claim, or moment of choice.

  2. 02Separate

    Evidence and tension: separate useful signals from category noise, polished claims, and obvious preferences.

  3. 03Name

    Translation: turn the pattern into language a team can remember and challenge.

  4. 04Translate

    Output: move the insight into product logic, positioning, messaging, CRM, or experience design.

  5. 05Make it travel

    Dossier: build the deck, framework, or phrase that lets the work keep moving.
