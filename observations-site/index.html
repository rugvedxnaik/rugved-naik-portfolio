Point of view

Products and marketing work when they start with the consumer.

A strong product is not just a feature set. Strong marketing is not just a message. Both work when they understand what people are trying to solve, signal, avoid, become, and remember.

My work sits between consumer insight, product thinking, and strategic storytelling. I look for the behavior or tension that makes an idea feel obvious once it is named.

What I read

Small consumer truths that can become product and marketing systems.

01 / identity

02 / ritual

03 / memory

04 / recognition

Luxury beauty strategy Two-week concept sprint

Face Architecture

A beauty territory that translates couture construction into product, service, ritual, and seasonal expression.

Consumer behavior

Beauty consumers use the face to construct identity through plane, line, volume, and accent.

Tension

Couture had authority, but beauty needed a repeatable consumer meaning beyond fashion adjacency.

Strategic move

Turn the face into architecture and make construction the organizing idea.

Output

Positioning, concept system, experience logic, and service cues.

Proof

Presented as a concept sprint to the Givenchy beauty team.

What it shows

Consumer identity can become a product and brand system.

Essay / product logic Framework

On Personalization

A product thinking framework for the difference between prediction, relevance, recognition, and identity.

Consumer behavior

People want products to feel attentive without reducing them to past clicks.

Tension

A product can predict someone accurately and still make them feel anonymous.

Strategic move

Separate usefulness from recognition and build toward identity.

Output

Framework development, product critique, and strategic writing.

Proof

Shows how thinking can become a usable product strategy tool.

What it shows

Consumer recognition can guide product and experience logic.

Method

The work moves from consumer truth to usable system.

  1. 01Observe

    Find the behavior, ritual, search pattern, category habit, or moment of choice.

  2. 02Name

    Turn the behavior into a clear consumer truth or tension.

  3. 03Translate

    Move the insight into product logic, positioning, messaging, or experience design.

  4. 04Structure

    Build the system so teams can use it, extend it, and explain it.

  5. 05Make memorable

    Give the work a phrase, format, or world that people can return to.
