A fragrance strategy about building differentiation where imitation cannot reach: emotional memory.

Context

Signal

Tension

A thinking system about the difference between prediction, relevance, recognition, and identity.

Prediction Relevance Recognition Identity
Context

Products increasingly equate personalization with recommendation accuracy.

Role

Framework development, product critique, strategic writing.

Signal

A product can predict someone perfectly and still feel anonymous.

Tension

Relevance removes friction, but recognition creates meaning.

System

A ladder from prediction to relevance to recognition to identity.

Proof
