Point of view

Consumer centric is not soft. It is operational.

A product decision asks what someone is trying to get done, signal, avoid, or become. A marketing decision asks what they should remember after the touchpoint disappears.

The case matters, but the consumer signal comes first. The company appears only after the behavior has been read and routed into a usable system.

Method

The method maps to the switchboard.

  1. 01Observe

    Find the behavior, ritual, review pattern, search language, claim, or moment of choice.

  2. 02Separate

    Evidence and tension: separate useful signals from category noise, polished claims, and obvious preferences.

  3. 03Name

    Translation: turn the pattern into language a team can remember and challenge.

  4. 04Translate

    Output: move the insight into product logic, positioning, messaging, CRM, or experience design.

  5. 05Make it travel

    Dossier: build the deck, framework, or phrase that lets the work keep moving.
