Givenchy Face Architecture

Role

Insight, concept, positioning, experience system

Outputs

Deck, beauty territory, consultation ritual, visual world

Proof

Signal

Givenchy owned couture authority, but beauty was not yet as ownable.

Fragrance strategy / PMM logic L'Oreal Brandstorm / 2026

The Permanence Paradox

A fragrance strategy that reframes luxury differentiation around emotional imprint when product-level cues are easy to copy.

Role

Competitive insight, narrative strategy, moat definition

Outputs

Concept deck, memory architecture, PMM-style strategic frame

Proof

Shows a strategic response to imitation beyond price, packaging, or message

Signal

A dupe can copy scent notes, bottle codes, and visual style.

Convention

The category treats dupes as a pricing or imitation problem.

Tension

If material cues can be copied, luxury needs a deeper layer of ownership.

Reframe

Luxury should not defend the bottle. It should own the memory the bottle creates.

System

A permanence architecture built around imprint formation, archive logic, emotional persistence, and ritualized return.

Proves

Strategic reframing, differentiation logic, and PMM-style moat thinking.

Thesis

Memory cannot be duped.

The next luxury battleground is emotional persistence.

Essay / Product logic Framework / ongoing

On Personalization

A product-thinking essay arguing that prediction, relevance, recognition, and identity are different levels of personalization.

Role

Framework development, product critique, strategic writing

Outputs

Concept ladder, evidence set, design implications

Proof

Shows how thinking can become a usable strategic tool

Signal

Products are getting better at prediction, but not always better at feeling personal.

Convention

Personalization is often defined by anticipating the next click, watch, listen, or purchase.

Tension

A product can be extremely relevant and still feel anonymous.

Reframe

Relevance is not recognition.

System

A ladder from prediction to relevance to recognition to identity.

Proves

Product thinking, framework creation, and sharp strategic writing.

Method

The simple structure behind the living archive.

It stays easy to follow because the same logic repeats. The words are simple; the work gets distinctive through the quality of the observation.

01

Signal

The overlooked behavior, contradiction, cue, ritual, or cultural shift.

02

Tension

The reason the category's current answer feels incomplete.

03

Distinction

The sharp line that changes how the problem is understood.

04

System

The repeatable structure: territory, ritual, message architecture, or product logic.

05

World

The visible form: deck, identity, interface, report, launch story, or experience.

Thinking Anchor

Relevance is not recognition.

The earlier portfolio had an important essay spine. This version keeps that depth, but makes it easier to scan: personalization is not one thing. It is a ladder from utility to identity.

01

Prediction

The system anticipates what comes next.

02

Relevance

The product becomes useful in the moment.

03

Recognition

The product reflects something meaningful back.

04

Identity

The product becomes part of how someone understands themselves.
