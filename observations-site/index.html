How it works

The work starts with what someone says, wants, avoids, or repeats. Open a signal to see how evidence becomes a system.

Read the labels as separate axes: route status shows whether the thinking has landed; proof status names the evidence level; the project line shows whether a dossier is available.

Point of view

Consumer centric is not soft. It is operational.

A product decision asks what someone is trying to get done, signal, avoid, or become. A marketing decision asks what they should remember after the touchpoint disappears.

The case matters, but the consumer signal comes first. The company appears only after the behavior has been read and translated into a usable system.

Method

The method maps to the archive.

  1. 01Observe

    Find the behavior, ritual, review pattern, search language, claim, or moment of choice.

  2. 02Separate

    Use the quote and evidence to separate useful signals from category noise and polished claims.

  3. 03Name

    Turn the pattern into a sentence a team can remember and challenge.

  4. 04Translate

    Use the connector moment to move from signal to product logic, positioning, CRM, or experience design.

  5. 05Make it travel

    Build the dossier, framework, or phrase that lets the work keep moving.
