Point of view

Consumer centric is not soft. It is operational.

A product decision asks what someone is trying to get done, signal, avoid, or become. A marketing decision asks what they should remember after the touchpoint disappears.

I work in that space between the person and the system: the behavior that explains the opportunity, the tension that makes the strategy necessary, and the structure that lets product, brand, and marketing move together.

Collectibility Over Loyalty

Why Miutine behaves more like a luxury object than a signature fragrance.

Evidence base

4,700+ reviews, competitive benchmarks, attribute-level review mining, and Instagram plus TikTok social listening.

Behavior

Consumers praised the bottle and object codes even when longevity weakened attachment.

Tension

Visibility and satisfaction were present, but identity drivers like confidence, sex appeal, and compliments were weaker.

Framework

Identity Value x Object Value separated collectible appeal from signature-fragrance attachment.

Output

Review mining, competitive read, social listening, framework, and recommendations.

Status

Full evidence-backed case now in the portfolio.

Applied to target house Active inquiry Beauty CRM and AI

What AI Can't Personalize

As AI personalizes discovery, beauty brands still need shared memory that stays recognizable.

Question

What must remain consistent for a beauty brand to stay recognizable across personalized journeys?

Rule

The closer something is to memory, the less it should be personalized. The closer something is to utility, the more it can adapt.

Behavior

Consumers want recognition without being reduced to past clicks.

Output

Brand Memory Architecture for separating adaptive utility from stable codes.

Status

Active independent inquiry for beauty, CRM, digital marketing, and brand equity measurement.

What it shows

Personalization strategy needs brand memory, not just targeting accuracy.

Shared Infrastructure, House-Specific Expression

Can luxury groups share customer intelligence without making every house feel the same?

Group question

Shared data can improve CRM, service, and relevance across a luxury portfolio.

Luxury risk

The same efficiency can flatten house memory if every brand starts behaving alike.

Distinction

This is group-level infrastructure thinking, distinct from Givenchy's house-level product territory.

System

Shared intelligence, house-specific expression.

Status

Active independent inquiry, with the next step focused on house codes and CRM use cases.

What it shows

Good data architecture has to protect meaning, not just increase efficiency.
